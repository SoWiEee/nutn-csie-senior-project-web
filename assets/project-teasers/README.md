Shared prompt: one landscape 16:9 flat minimal research schematic, with 5–8 main shapes, broad flat color areas and consistent thin strokes. Solid midnight navy background (#0B2248), muted blue fills, pale blue outlines and white; a restrained burgundy accent. Centered subjects remain readable within a shallow middle crop. No gradients, realistic texture, noise, glow, shadows, cinematic lighting, detailed faces, text, logos, numerical claims or watermarks. WebP assets are normalized to 1280 × 720 with a shared exact background color.
